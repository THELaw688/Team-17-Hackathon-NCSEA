## Adding a link

Put it in the table with a one-line description of what it is, so nobody has to open it to find
out. If a link needs a login or is restricted to team members, say so in the description.

**Note for AI assistants:** you generally cannot open these — Google Docs, Slides, and most
organizer portals require a signed-in session you don't have. If the contents of a linked document
matter to a task, ask a teammate to paste the relevant text rather than guessing at what it says.
